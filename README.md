# 🏎️ Super Cars II VR (Meta Quest 3)

A high-fidelity WebXR 3D combat racing remake inspired by the classic Commodore Amiga game **Super Cars II** (Gremlin Graphics, 1991).

Built with **Three.js** and **WebXR**, featuring PBR metallic car paints, high-detail race circuit with an elevated jump ramp, competitive AI racers, twin front-mounted rockets, procedural Web Audio, and multiple VR camera perspectives.

---

## 🥽 How to Play on Meta Quest 3

1. Put on your **Meta Quest 3** headset.
2. Open the **Meta Quest Browser**.
3. Navigate to the GitHub Pages link:
   **`https://fredfesty.github.io/super-cars-vr/`**
4. Click the **"ENTER VR (QUEST 3)"** button at the bottom of the screen.
5. You'll be placed directly on the starting grid in an elevated 3D tabletop/drone perspective overlooking your car and the race track!

---

## 🎮 Controls

### Meta Quest 3 Touch Controllers:
* **Left Thumbstick (Left / Right)**: Steer car.
* **Right Trigger / Right Thumbstick**: Throttle & Brake / Reverse.
* **'A' Button / Right Grip**: Fire twin front missiles 🚀.
* **'Y' Button / Left Grip**: Switch Camera Mode (Drone Top-Down, Third-Person Chase, Bumper).
* **Haptics**: Rumble feedback on wall impacts, rival bumps, and missile launches.

### Desktop Browser (Keyboard & Gamepad):
* **`W` / `S`** or **`Up` / `Down`**: Throttle & Reverse / Brake.
* **`A` / `D`** or **`Left` / `Right`**: Steer.
* **`Space`**: Fire twin front missiles 🚀.
* **`C`**: Switch Camera View (Drone / Chase / Bumper).
* **`R`**: Reset race grid.
* **Gamepad**: Standard Xbox / PlayStation controller fully supported (Left Stick steer, RT gas, LT brake, A fire, Y camera).

---

## 🏁 Game Features

* **Instant Race Action**: Start directly on the grid against 3 AI opponents (Electric Blue, Crimson Red, Canary Yellow, Viper Green).
* **Classic Super Cars II Combat**: Fire dual rockets at competitors ahead. Direct hits trigger 360-degree spin-outs with fire and smoke.
* **Jump Ramp**: Full 3D elevated ramp that launches your car airborne over track gaps with suspension compression upon landing.
* **Multiple VR Camera Modes**:
  1. **Drone / Top-Down (Classic)**: Experience the original bird's-eye view transformed into a stunning 3D miniature tabletop diorama in VR.
  2. **Third-Person Chase Cam**: High-speed trailing perspective.
  3. **Bumper / Hood Cam**: Close-to-the-asphalt adrenaline view.
* **PBR 3D Graphics**: Clearcoat metallic car paint, asphalt texture, 3D red/white kerbs, Armco guardrails, floodlights, grandstands, and soft shadows.
* **Procedural Web Audio**: Real-time dual-oscillator engine roar, tire screeches, impact crunches, and explosion booms without any external asset latency.
